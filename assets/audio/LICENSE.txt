Rift Convoy audio sources
==========================

The sound effects in this folder come from official Kenney starter-kit
repositories. Kenney states that the included sound effects are released
under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0), so they may be used, modified and
distributed without attribution.

Sources verified and retrieved on 2026-10-12:

- Kenney Starter Kit FPS
  https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS
  Weapon mappings (processed locally into unique OGG files):
  - blaster_repeater.ogg -> weapon-gun.ogg
  - jump_c.ogg -> weapon-frost.ogg
  - jump_a.ogg + enemy_destroy.ogg -> weapon-rocket.ogg
  - blaster.ogg -> weapon-laser.ogg
  - enemy_attack.ogg -> weapon-tesla.ogg
  - weapon_change.ogg -> weapon-repair.ogg

  The processing only changes timing, pitch, filtering, echo, mixing, and
  loudness. Every weapon uses a different final file and sonic profile.

- Kenney Starter Kit City Builder
  https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder
  Used: placement-a.ogg, toggle.ogg
