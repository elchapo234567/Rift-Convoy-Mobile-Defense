Mega Tower Defense audio sources
=================================

The sound effects in this folder come from official Kenney starter-kit
repositories. Kenney states that the included sound effects are released
under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0), so they may be used, modified and
distributed without attribution.

Sources retrieved on 2026-10-09:

- Kenney Starter Kit FPS
  https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS
  Used: blaster.ogg, blaster_repeater.ogg, enemy_attack.ogg,
  enemy_destroy.ogg, land.ogg, weapon_change.ogg

- Kenney Starter Kit City Builder
  https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder
  Used: placement-a.ogg, toggle.ogg

License declaration:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The files were renamed for their purpose in this game. No attribution is
required, but this notice is retained to document provenance.
