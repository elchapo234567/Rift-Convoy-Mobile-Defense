# Asset provenance

## Game artwork

The enemy, module, vehicle, aircraft, battlefield, and earlier interface artwork was created for the user's previous `Tower-defens` project and intentionally reused as source material for this new game with the user's authorization.

The new convoy menu artwork, three marketing covers, four road-free terrain backgrounds (forest, ice, volcano, and void), four orthographic convoy vehicles, and all 24 top-down module upgrade designs were generated specifically for **Rift Convoy: Mobile Defense** with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-12. The generated originals are retained by the development workspace; isolated and optimized derivatives are stored in this repository. Vehicle chassis and module art are rendered as separate layers so their position, rotation, upgrade state, and destruction always stay synchronized.

No third-party game names, characters, logos, or copyrighted franchise identifiers are intentionally used.

## Audio

The bundled sound effects are from official Kenney starter-kit repositories and are released under Creative Commons Zero 1.0. Full source details are retained in `assets/audio/LICENSE.txt`.
